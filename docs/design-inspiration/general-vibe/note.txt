I really like the vibe and aesthetic of Meta's Edit app. The direct companion app for Instagram.

It's very basic. You log in, and then you have a view of all your projects. You can create a new project, and it kind of has just those two modes.

The view project screen is white, and when you're editing your media and everything other than doing your projects, the screen is black. It kind of has that inverse: when you're working on it, it's black, versus when you're just viewing your products on the app, it's white.
