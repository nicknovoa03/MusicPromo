Flyer · Voice (event-specific extensions to MusicPromo voice)

Do

✓Type does the shouting. 96px Anton is loud enough — never an exclamation point on a flyer.
✓Abbreviate dates. SAT APR 25, not Saturday, April 25th 2026.
✓Em dash for time ranges. 4PM – 8PM, not 4-8PM or 4 to 8.
✓Lowercase + slashes for genre lines. house / disco / grooves.
✓Pipe-separate footer. SAT APR 25 | 4PM–8PM | NEON GROTTO.
✓Interpunct in eyebrows. HAPPY HOUR · 4–7PM.

Don't

✗DISCO AT DUSK !!! — Type already carries weight.
✗Saturday April 25th, 2026 at 4:00pm — Too long, too formal.
✗4-8pm — Wrong dash, wrong case.
✗House, Disco & Grooves Mix — Title-case adds nothing.
✗Save the date 🎉 — No emoji, ever.
✗Come party with us! — Marketing voice. Flyers don't beg.
